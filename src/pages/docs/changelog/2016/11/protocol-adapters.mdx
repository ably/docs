Ably now supports Pusher, PubNub, AMQP, STOMP protocols (MQTT coming soon...) We're excited to announce [Ably Protocol Adapters](https://ably.com/adapters).

Our vision for realtime is no protocol lock-in and interoperability across protocols.

As of today we support Pusher, PubNub, AMQP and STOMP. MQTT and other open protocols will be added soon as we pursue our protocol agnostic approach.
