The [session](/docs/ai-transport/concepts/sessions) is the durable conversation that clients and agents attach to, and it outlives any connection. A [run](/docs/ai-transport/concepts/runs) is one turn of agent work inside a session, with its own identity, lifecycle, and end reason. The [conversation tree](/docs/ai-transport/concepts/conversation-tree) is how the session's messages are organised, which is what lets a user edit or regenerate a message without losing what came before.
