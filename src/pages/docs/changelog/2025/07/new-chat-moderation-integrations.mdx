We are excited to announce two new integrations for text moderation in Ably Chat. [Tisane](https://tisane.ai/) and [Bodyguard](https://www.bodyguard.ai/en) are platforms that bring powerful AI-driven moderation to your application, allowing you to detect and block harmful content before it reaches your users. The flexible integration with Ably Chat allows you to get up and running in minutes, with moderation thresholds customised to suit your community requirements.

For more information, check out the [moderation docs](https://ably.com/docs/chat/moderation). To get started for free and set up the integrations, log in to your Ably dashboard.
